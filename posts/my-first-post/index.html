Performance measurement

December 28, 2025 · 1 min · Harsh Mishra

I was reading the book “AI : A Modern Approach” and one of the interesting quote there on agents was - “As a general rule, it is better to design performance measures according to what one actually wants to be achieved in the environment, rather than according to how one thinks the agent should behave”

I believe the above can be applied to any product or feature you develop. Lot of the time we think about whether our product is behaving the way we want it to behave but miss whether it’s achieveing the results we want it to achieve.
