title = 'Should you fight opinions?'

I watched an LP concert. One friend said the new vocalist isn't good -  "how can you even listen to her?" That bothered me because I liked the concert and loved her singing. I thought about getting into a debate with him and trying to convince him she's better. But then I realized it's just an opinion based on personal taste and experience, not facts. That means no matter what I say, I can't really convince him, and he can't convince me. Then I realised, The only opinions worth arguing are ones grounded in facts or strong hypotheses from real experience and taste in music or taste in art isn't one of them.
